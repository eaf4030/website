This is my first post. Each blog post is just a text file in the `_posts`
folder, named like `YEAR-MONTH-DAY-short-title.md`.

You can write in plain text with simple formatting:

- **Bold** with `**double stars**`
- *Italics* with `*single stars*`
- Links like `[this](https://example.com)`

> And quotes, with a `>` at the start of the line.

Delete this post whenever you're ready to write your own.
